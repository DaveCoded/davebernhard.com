I'm looking to develop my understanding and skills in web design. So I looked a bit closer
at an app I use every day: Todoist. This is really an exercise in developing my eye and
learning to see. I didn't have any criteria in mind. These are just some of the things I noticed when I had a spare
half hour.

## Obscure menu

On the reporting page, there's a list of filters under the page's heading.
There is also a choice of 5 "presets" hidden away in this menu.
You have to click on the heading - or adjacent carret icon button - to find it.

<Image src={reportingMenu} alt="" />

I don't use the reporting page so it's meaningless to say that I've never seen this
before, but I think I would never have found it if I weren't looking intentionally.
I'm also not sure what it's for. "All activity" effectively means "no filters", in which
case a "clear filters" button would work.

"My completed tasks", "Recently completed tasks" and "Recently added tasks" would be well served by a combination of filtering and ordering:
"completed tasks" or "added tasks" + order by most recent.

I think that just leaves "My favorites" as a special case that is not served by the other filters.
